# Hey 👋

I'm **Athul Cyriac Ajay**, a ***Site Reliability Engineer*** from Kerala, India. I love working with infrastructure, writing code, and solving interesting problems.

I currently work at **[Ultrahuman](https://ultrahuman.com)(12/2025 - )**, where I'm part of the team building and maintaining the infrastructure behind their metabolic health platform and the ring.

Before this, I was an **SRE at [KnowBe4](https://knowbe4.com)(01/2024 - 12/2025)** in Release Engineering, where I worked on distributed systems, managed GitLab infrastructure, and helped teams ship code faster and more reliably.

Prior to that, I spent a couple of years at **[Frappe](https://frappe.io)(07/2022 - 12/2023)** as a ***Platform Engineer***, working on cloud infrastructure and helping make **ERPNext deployments smoother** for their customers.

Along the way, I got to work on a few interesting things—like implementing **GitLab Geo across regions** and building internal systems to track **AI adoption metrics** at KnowBe4, **Managed Hosting** and functional automations at Frappe.

I enjoy building tools and contributing to **open source**. Some projects that people seem to find useful include **[Waka-Readme](https://github.com/athul/waka-readme)** (a GitHub Action that shows coding stats on your profile with ***1,700+ stars***), **[Archie](https://github.com/athul/archie)** (the minimal Hugo theme that powers this site with ***1,200+ stars***), and **[Shelby](https://github.com/athul/shelby)** (a fast shell prompt written in Go with ***180+ stars***). Check out more of my [Projects](/projects) or my [Resume](/resume) if you're curious about the technical details.

I primarily write **Python** and **Go**, work a lot with **AWS** and **Terraform**, and enjoy building things with **FastAPI**, and **Vue**. Most of my day involves _Docker containers_, _Kubernetes_, _CI/CD_ and various _monitoring tools_.

I love exploring Kerala on weekend bike/car trips, watching movies, reading books and sometimes just sleeping in. I also write about my projects and experiences on my [blog](/blog). It's mostly year end reviews on my blog these days but I try to write more.

I graduated with a **B.Tech in Computer Science** from [College of Engineering Kidangoor](https://ce-kgr.org) under [APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University](https://ktu.edu.in) in 2022, where I was fortunate to receive the ***Best Outgoing Student Award***.
